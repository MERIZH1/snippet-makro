How Snippy works

A quick guide to the main features.

Copy a snippetClick the left side of an item. Its text is copied to the clipboard.
Copy a memoClick Memo on the right. The memo is copied separately.
TranslateChoose a target language and click a snippet. Edge/Chrome translates on this device. The language model may need a one-time download. Memos are never translated.
HyperlinksPaste text from an email or webpage with Ctrl+V: linked words keep their links. Or enter link text and an address, then click Insert link.
Add snippetsClick ＋ to save a name, folder, text, and optional memo.
FoldersClick a folder heading to expand or collapse it. Unfiled snippets appear at the top.
ReorderDrag an item using ⠿ to move it or place it in a folder.
Floating windowThe window icon opens the copy view above other apps. The order stays the same.
ContactsClick a phone number to copy it. Contacts can be edited at any time.
BackupsOpen ⚙ to export all data or import a JSON backup.
Offline useAfter the first online visit, Snippy is saved on this device and works without the server or an internet connection. Each device has its own data.
Your dataSnippets, contacts, and settings stay in this browser on this device. They are not sent to other users. Export a backup in ⚙ before changing devices.

Settings

Automatic detection is the default. If a snippet is too short to identify reliably, choose its source language here. Invalid translation codes are never copied.
